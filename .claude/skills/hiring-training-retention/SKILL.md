# Hiring, training & retention design

## Roles (`docs/06-staffing.md`)
General manager · assistant manager/lead handler · dog handlers/playroom attendants · front desk/client care · bather/groomer · overnight attendant (if boarding) · trainer (contract).

## Job description template
Title · reports to · purpose (1 line) · key duties · physical requirements (lifting 50 lb, standing, noise, heat, bites and scratches risk) · must-haves (reliability, calm under pressure, dog experience) · nice-to-haves (bilingual EN/ES, pet CPR, vet assistant training) · schedule · pay range · growth path.

## Hiring process
1. Post on Indeed, local vet and vet-tech schools (Miami Dade College, vocational programs), UM/FIU job boards, pet community groups, Instagram.
2. Phone screen (availability, reliability, dog experience).
3. **Working interview** (1–2 paid hours in a calm group): watch how they read dogs, follow instructions and work with others.
4. Reference and background checks (and driving record for transport roles). Complete I-9 (and E-Verify if 25+ employees).

## Training curriculum (first 2 weeks + ongoing)
- Days 1–2: safety rules, facility tour, SOPs (check-in, escape prevention, cleaning), shadowing.
- Days 3–7: dog body language, playgroup management, intervention ladder (`temperament-playgroup-management`), heat protocol.
- Week 2: supervised group lead; **pet CPR and first aid**; disease and cleaning protocols; incident reporting.
- Certification options: Dog Daycare Academy, Dogs Playing for Life, Pet Care Services Association programs, PACCC credentials, pet first aid (Red Cross, PetTech).
- Ongoing: monthly refreshers, incident reviews, twice-yearly hurricane and fire drills.

## Retention
Pay above minimum wage with clear steps (handler I → II → lead → trainer/manager); consistent schedules published 2+ weeks ahead; free daycare for staff dogs; a recognition and safety bonus tied to zero-incident months and reviews; heat relief (break room, hydration); exit interviews. Track **turnover %**, time-to-fill, and training completion.

## Output
JDs, a hiring funnel and timeline, the training plan, retention budget, and HR KPIs.
