# Emergency & business continuity planning

## Hurricane plan (Atlantic season 1 June–30 November)
- **Pre-season (May):** check the generator, fuel contract, shutters or impact glass, roof and drains; update client emergency contacts; confirm insurance (named-storm deductible, flood); build the supply kit (food, medications, cleaning supplies, water, crates, first aid, battery radios, records offline).
- **Triggers (Estimate, adjust to the site's evacuation zone):**
  - Tropical storm or hurricane **watch** for Miami-Dade → notify clients, stop new boarding check-ins, start the prep checklist.
  - Hurricane **warning** or an evacuation order covering the site → close daycare and send boarders home or to a pre-arranged inland partner facility. Never leave animals unattended.
  - After the storm: safety inspection (power, water, flooding, debris in yards, fence damage) before reopening.
- **Boarding dogs:** the boarding agreement must include an emergency plan and owner authorisation.

## Heat (summer heat index often >100°F)
Indoor play stays within Miami-Dade's 50–85°F rule. Outdoor sessions only in the early morning or evening. Cap outdoor time by heat index (e.g. none above ~90°F heat index for high-risk dogs; brachycephalic breeds indoors only). Turf temperature checks, shade, water, cooling mats. Train staff to recognise heatstroke, and have an emergency cooling protocol plus vet transport.

## Power and AC loss
A generator or transfer switch sized for HVAC and critical loads. If AC cannot keep the temperature ≤ 85°F, start the closure and owner-pickup protocol.

## Outbreak closure
Follow `disease-prevention-vaccine-policy`: notify owners, temporarily close or restrict group play, deep-disinfect, coordinate with vets and Animal Services, and plan a reopening.

## Other scenarios
Fire and evacuation (evacuation routes for leashed dogs, slip leads at exits, a muster point away from traffic) · escaped or lost dog (search protocol, notify the owner immediately, microchip records, local lost-pet networks) · serious injury or death (vet transport, owner notification script, incident report, insurance notice) · active threat · IT/booking outage (paper check-in backup).

## Crisis communications
Prepare SMS/email/social templates for closure, an incident, and reopening, in English and Spanish. One spokesperson. Facts first; no speculation.

## Output
A plan document with triggers → actions → owner → checklist, the drill schedule (twice a year), and the gaps that need capex or insurance.
