# Zoning & permit pathway mapping

## Standard sequence (`docs/05-permits-licenses.md`; confirm per jurisdiction)
| # | Step | Agency (typical) | Depends on | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entity, EIN, fictitious name | Sunbiz, IRS | — | Can be done in parallel |
| 2 | **Zoning verification / written use determination** | City Planning & Zoning (e.g. City of Miami, Miami 21 Art. 4 Table 3) or Miami-Dade RER (unincorporated) | Parcel identified | **Do this before signing the lease** (R = by right, W = Warrant, E = Exception with a hearing) |
| 3 | Lease signed with contingencies | Landlord | 2 | |
| 4 | Design drawings (architect, MEP) | — | 3 | |
| 5 | Building permit (plumbing, mechanical, electrical, structural) | City or county Building Dept. | 4 | Change of use / occupancy |
| 6 | Fire plan review | Fire Rescue (city or county) | 4 | Sprinklers, alarm, egress |
| 7 | DERM / WASD review (sanitary connection, interceptor, waste) | Miami-Dade RER/DERM, WASD | 4 | Floor drains |
| 8 | Sign permit | City | 3 | |
| 9 | Construction and inspections → **Certificate of Occupancy / Completion** | Building Dept. | 5–7 | |
| 10 | **Certificate of Use (CU)** | City (Miami 21 Art. 7 §7.1.2.1) or county | 9 | Required **before opening**; often renewed annually |
| 11 | **Local Business Tax Receipts: city + county** | City finance; Miami-Dade Tax Collector | 10 (often needs the CU) | Tax year 1 Oct–30 Sep |
| 12 | Florida DOR sales tax and reemployment tax registration | FDOR | 1 | |
| 13 | Workers' comp in force (4+ employees) | Carrier | Hiring | |
| 14 | Alarm registration (if monitored) | City or county | 9 | |
| — | Animal Services | Miami-Dade | — | No pet care center licence currently issued (per county), but Ch. 5 standards apply. **Confirm.** |

## Method
1. Identify the jurisdiction and zoning district of the parcel.
2. Confirm how the use is classified (daytime pet care center vs. kennel/boarding) and whether it is allowed (R/W/E/prohibited).
3. Fill in the table for that jurisdiction with links, fees (if found) and expected durations. Mark unknown durations as risks.
4. Identify the critical path (usually zoning → drawings → building permit → inspections → CU).
5. List the documents needed at each step.

## Output
A jurisdiction-specific pathway table, the critical path, risks (hearings, neighbour objections, sewer capacity), and the calls to make this week.
