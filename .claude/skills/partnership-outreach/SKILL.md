# Partnership & community outreach

## Partner types and value exchange
| Partner | What they get | What we get |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury rental and condo towers (Brickell, Edgewater, Wynwood, Doral) | A resident perk, lobby events, a pet amenity without the cost | Resident discounts drive volume; a possible shuttle pickup stop |
| Veterinary clinics | A trusted place to refer clients; vaccine-compliance partner | Referrals; health credibility; an emergency vet relationship |
| Groomers, trainers, pet stores | Cross-referrals, shared events | Referrals; retail and product partners |
| Employers and coworking spaces | A pet-friendly perk for staff | Corporate memberships |
| Rescues and breeders | A "welcome home" pack for adopters and buyers | Puppy-program leads; goodwill |
| Local creators, pet-friendly restaurants | Content and events | Reach and launch buzz |

## Outreach sequence
1. Build a target list (name, contact, address, size such as unit count, fit score).
2. Warm intro or a personalised email or DM with a one-paragraph offer.
3. Follow up at day 4 and day 10, with an in-person drop-in and treats.
4. Pilot: 60–90 days with a tracked code. Review the results, then formalise.

## Offer guardrails
Keep the discount ≤ 10–15% so the realised price stays ≥ $38/day (`pricing-package-design`). Prefer added value (a free evaluation, a first bath) over deep discounts. Track every partner code.

## Output
A prioritised partner list, pitch scripts (EN/ES), offer terms, a tracking sheet layout, and success metrics (leads, conversions, revenue per partner).
