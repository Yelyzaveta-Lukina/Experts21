# Contract, waiver & policy drafting review

> Research support only. A Florida-licensed attorney must finalise every document.

## Client service agreement: clause checklist
1. Parties, the dog(s), services, prices and payment authorisation (card on file, packages, refunds and expiry).
2. **Eligibility**: vaccines (rabies required by F.S. 828.30; plus our policy: DHPP, Bordetella, canine influenza, leptospirosis), spay/neuter by ~6–7 months, flea and tick prevention, temperament evaluation.
3. **Health declaration**: the owner must disclose illness, aggression history and medications. The facility may refuse dogs with known or suspected contagious disease (Miami-Dade Ch. 5 requires this).
4. **Assumption of risk for group play** (scuffles, scratches, illness transmission, injury), written in plain language.
5. **Release / limitation of liability.** Florida courts generally enforce clear and unambiguous exculpatory clauses for ordinary negligence, but not for gross negligence or intentional acts. The attorney must confirm current case law and wording.
6. **Emergency veterinary authorisation** and the owner's responsibility for costs (**required by Ch. 5**: medical emergency release form). Name the preferred vet and a spending cap.
7. Right to remove or ban a dog. Incident notification process.
8. Photo and video consent (social media, webcams).
9. Late pickup, abandonment and unclaimed-dog procedure.
10. Bite liability: the owner is responsible for their own dog's behaviour (F.S. 767.04 makes owners strictly liable for bites). An indemnity clause should be considered.
11. Governing law (Florida), venue (Miami-Dade), dispute resolution, severability.
12. Electronic signature; versioning; a Spanish translation for information only, with English controlling (or attorney's choice).

## Policies to have in writing
Vaccine and health policy · incident and injury policy · weather and hurricane closure policy · cancellation and refund policy · privacy and webcam policy · staff code of conduct.

## Lease and vendor review
Use the clause list in `commercial-lease-analysis`. For vendor/SaaS contracts check data ownership, auto-renewal and termination.

## Review method
1. List the clauses present or missing against the checklist.
2. Flag ambiguous, one-sided or unenforceable-looking language.
3. Propose plain-English replacement text, marked "draft for attorney review".
4. Note conflicts with insurance policy conditions (hand off to the insurance and risk manager).
