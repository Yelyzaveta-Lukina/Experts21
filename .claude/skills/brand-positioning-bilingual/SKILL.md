# Brand positioning & bilingual messaging

## Positioning options (`docs/07-marketing.md`)
A. **"Safe, transparent, climate-smart" premium daycare** (recommended start): small supervised groups, published ratios, certified staff, live cams, indoor AC play built for Miami heat. $42–50/day.
B. Neighbourhood value daycare: $30–38/day.
C. Resort/lifestyle: $50+/day, competes with D.O.G.

## Positioning statement template
For **[segment]** who **[need]**, **[brand]** is the **[category]** that **[key benefit]** because **[proof points]**, unlike **[alternative]**.

## Message pillars and proof points
1. **Safety you can see**: ratio (e.g. 1:12), temperament evaluations, pet-CPR certified staff, incident transparency, webcams.
2. **Built for Miami**: air-conditioned indoor play, heat protocols, hurricane plan.
3. **Fits your day**: 7am–7pm, quick drop-off, online booking, memberships.
4. **Happy, tired dog**: daily report cards and photos.

## Segment angles (`docs/02-target-customers.md`)
Brickell professional → convenience and guilt-free workdays · Gables/Doral family → trust, cleanliness, Spanish-speaking staff · Puppy owners → socialisation and training · Travellers → boarding with daytime play.

## Bilingual guidelines
- Use English and Spanish everywhere customers look: website, signage, forms, social, staff greetings.
- Spanish: neutral Latin American Spanish suited to Miami's Cuban, Venezuelan, Colombian and other communities. Use "guardería canina" / "daycare para perros", "hospedaje" (boarding) and "baño y peluquería" (grooming). Use "usted" in forms and policies and the friendlier "tú" on social media.
- Have a native speaker review the copy. Avoid literal translations of puns.

## Naming checklist
Easy to say in both languages; .com domain and social handles available; no conflict on USPTO TESS, the Florida trademark register or Sunbiz names; not confusingly similar to local competitors (Dogtown, D.O.G., Bark Square...).

## Output
A positioning statement, pillars with proof, a tagline shortlist, sample EN/ES copy (homepage hero, Google Business description, flyer), and the naming checks.
