# Disease prevention & vaccine policy

> A licensed veterinarian should approve the final policy.

## Vaccine and health requirements (policy draft)
| Item | Requirement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Rabies | Required, current (dogs ≥ 4 months) | **Law**: F.S. 828.30; Miami-Dade licence tag |
| DHPP / DAPP | Required, current | Industry standard |
| Bordetella (kennel cough) | Required (often every 6–12 months) | Industry standard |
| Canine influenza (H3N2/H3N8) | Strongly recommended or required | Public health guidance after daycare outbreaks |
| Leptospirosis | Strongly recommended or required | LA 2021 outbreak: ~66% of cases had daycare/boarding exposure; Florida lepto risk all year |
| Fecal test | Negative within 6–12 months | Parasites (giardia etc.) |
| Flea/tick prevention | Required | |
| Puppies | Full puppy series complete before group play | |

Verify records directly from the vet. Store expiry dates in the booking software with automatic alerts.

## Daily health check at drop-off
Ask the owner: coughing, sneezing, vomiting, diarrhoea, lethargy, appetite, new medications? Look for: nasal or eye discharge, cough, limping, wounds, skin issues, fleas. **Miami-Dade Ch. 5: do not accept any dog with a known or suspected communicable disease.**

## Exclusion / return rules (Estimate, vet to confirm)
- Cough or respiratory signs → home; return after symptom-free for 7–10 days or with vet clearance.
- Vomiting or diarrhoea → home; return after 48–72 hours symptom-free.
- Females in heat → excluded.

## Isolation
A separate room (ideally separate air) for a dog that becomes ill during the day. Call the owner for immediate pickup. Disinfect the room after use.

## Cleaning and disinfection
- Spot-clean waste immediately. Daily full clean of floors and surfaces. Wash bowls, toys and bedding daily (Ch. 5: clean enclosures after each dog leaves).
- Use disinfectants effective against parvovirus and respiratory pathogens (e.g. accelerated hydrogen peroxide, potassium peroxymonosulfate, or bleach at the label dilution). **Respect the label contact time.** Clean before disinfecting.
- Outdoor yards: avoid standing water and wildlife access (lepto); disinfect and rinse turf; manage drainage.
- Staff hygiene: handwashing between groups; PPE for illness handling. Leptospirosis can infect people (zoonotic).

## Outbreak response
Triggers: ≥ 2–3 dogs with similar signs within a week, or an alert from Animal Services or local vets (Miami-Dade issued CIRDC/canine pneumovirus warnings in 2022).
Steps: notify the owners of exposed dogs → restrict or close group play (consider 1–2 weeks) → deep disinfection → consult the vet and the county → require vet clearance to return → communicate transparently (templates in `emergency-continuity-planning`) → review the cause.
