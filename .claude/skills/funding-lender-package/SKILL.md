# Funding & lender package preparation

## Sources of capital
| Source | Use | Notes (verify current terms) |
|---|---|---|
| SBA 7(a) | Build-out, equipment, working capital, franchise fee | Up to $5M; lenders typically want ~10–30% owner equity injection, collateral if available, and a personal guarantee |
| SBA 504 | Buying real estate or major fixed assets | If buying the building |
| Conventional bank / credit union | Smaller loans | Often needs stronger collateral |
| Equity (owner, friends and family, investors) | Equity injection, reserves | Dilution; investor memo |
| Landlord TI allowance | Build-out | Negotiate first; reduces debt (`commercial-lease-analysis`) |
| Equipment leasing / vendor financing | Kennels, grooming, HVAC | Higher cost, preserves cash |
| Franchisor-referred lenders | Franchise route | Dogtopia advertises third-party financing relationships |

## Lender package contents
Executive summary · owner bios and relevant experience · market and competitor analysis (`docs/01`–`03`) · site and lease (LOI) · build-out budget and GC bids · **36-month projections with assumptions** (from the financial modeler) · sources and uses table · break-even and downside case · personal financial statements, tax returns, credit · collateral list · insurance plan · permit pathway.

## Independent vs franchise
| Factor | Independent | Franchise (e.g. Dogtopia) |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront | Lean $165–370k / Standard $525k–$1.2M (Estimate) | $608k–$1.27M (company); 2026 FDD reported $664k–$1.48M |
| Ongoing fees | None | 7% royalty + 2% brand fund (+ tech fees) |
| Brand, systems, training | Build your own | Provided |
| Lender comfort | Lower | Higher (SBA franchise directory) |
| Flexibility | Full | Bound by agreement, territory |
| Requirements | — | Dogtopia: ~$1M net worth, ~$200k liquid (Reported) |
Review **FDD Item 5/6** (fees), **7** (initial investment), **19** (financial performance), **20** (outlets opening, closing and transfers: a closure rate is a red flag), **21** (franchisor financials). Have a franchise attorney review it.

## Output
A sources and uses table, a recommended capital stack, a debt-service coverage check (DSCR ≥ 1.25× at the base case), a package checklist with gaps, and the franchise vs independent recommendation.
