# Pet-business tech stack selection

## Must-have requirements
Online booking and client portal/app · memberships, packages and auto-billing · **vaccine record upload with expiry alerts** · digital waivers and e-signature · check-in/out (tablet, QR) · playgroup and capacity tracking · report cards with photos · incident logging · grooming and boarding calendars · retail POS · reporting (dogs per day, revenue per dog-day, utilisation, churn) · staff scheduling or integration · SMS/email marketing · QuickBooks/Xero integration · English/Spanish client communication.

## Candidates to evaluate (verify current features and pricing)
Gingr · MoeGo · PetExec · Kennel Connection · Revelation Pets · DaySmart Pet (formerly 123Pet) · PawPartner. Webcams: dedicated pet-cam platforms or IP cameras with a secure viewer. Payments: built-in processor vs Stripe/Square (compare effective rate ~2.6–3.0%).

## Evaluation matrix
`Vendor | Must-haves met (x/14) | Ease of use (staff trial) | Client app quality | Spanish support | Integrations | Price (setup + monthly + payments) | Contract terms and data export | Support hours | Score`

## Method
1. Confirm the requirements with operations and marketing.
2. Shortlist 3. Run demos with a scripted scenario (new client → eval → membership → check-in → incident → report card → monthly invoice).
3. Check data ownership and export, uptime, and how client personal data is protected.
4. Budget (Estimate): $100–400/month software + payments ~3% of revenue + webcams $5–20k setup + website $3–10k.

## Output
A requirements list, comparison matrix, recommendation, implementation timeline (go live 4–6 weeks before opening for the waitlist), and integration diagram (text).
