# Canine temperament assessment & playgroup management

## Intake evaluation (30–60 minutes)
1. Paperwork: vaccines, spay/neuter status, health and behaviour history, bite history.
2. Neutral greeting with staff (handling paws, ears, collar grab).
3. Introduction to **one calm, socially fluent "helper" dog** through a barrier, then off-leash.
4. Small group of 3–5 dogs of similar size and energy.
5. Score: sociability, play style, arousal and recovery, response to interruption, resource guarding, noise sensitivity.
6. Outcome: **accept** (which group) / **accept with conditions** (half-days, small group) / **decline** (with a referral to a trainer).

**Usually decline:** a history of serious bites, high arousal with no recovery, intense resource guarding around other dogs, intact dogs older than ~6–7 months, dogs that are fearful and shut down after several attempts.

## Playgroups
- Group by **size and play style**: large/rough, medium/moderate, small/calm, puppies, seniors.
- Ratios: **1:10–12 for large or high-energy groups; up to 1:15 for calm or small dogs. A person is always present** (`docs/06-staffing.md`).
- Group size cap ~20–25 per room, even if staffing allows more.
- Mandatory **midday rest** (1–2 hours crated or in a quiet room). Over-tired dogs fight more.
- Manage entry: introduce new arrivals one at a time; no crowding at the gate.

## Body language: watch for
Stiffening, hard stare, freezing, raised hackles with stiff movement, lip curl, a "T-position" over another dog's shoulders, mounting, ganging up, the same dog always being chased, the chased dog trying to hide. Healthy play: loose bouncy bodies, play bows, role reversals, self-handicapping, pauses.

## Intervention ladder
Verbal cue/interrupt → body block → redirect or leash out for a short time-out → separate the group or rotate dogs → **fight protocol**: never put hands between dogs; use noise, a spray bottle or citronella, a board or barrier, then a two-person wheelbarrow separation as a last resort; then check both dogs and staff, and file the incident report.

## Incident levels
L1 scuffle with no injury (log) · L2 minor scratch (first aid, owner informed at pickup) · L3 puncture or vet visit (owner called immediately, manager review, consider removal) · L4 serious injury (emergency vet, insurance notice, formal review).

## Output
An evaluation form, group definitions, the ratio and staffing implications, and training content for handlers.
