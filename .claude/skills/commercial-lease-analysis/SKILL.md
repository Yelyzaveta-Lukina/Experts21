# Commercial lease analysis

## Benchmarks (`docs/04-costs.md`)
Miami industrial asking rent ~$16.2–16.8/sq ft NNN (2025, Verified), ~$17.5 in Q1 2026. Flex space ~$20–22 (Reported). Vacancy is ~6% (a tight market, so landlords have leverage).

## Effective occupancy cost
`Annual cost = (base rent + NNN) × sq ft`. Spread free rent and TI over the term:
`Effective rent = (Σ rent over term − free rent − TI allowance beyond tenant cost) ÷ (sq ft × years)`. Compare spaces on **effective all-in $/sq ft** plus your share of the build-out.

## Must-have terms for a dog daycare
1. **Use clause** that names "dog daycare, boarding, grooming, training and related retail", including noise and odour inherent to the use.
2. **Contingency**: the tenant can walk away if zoning, permits or the Certificate of Use are not obtained within X days.
3. **Rent commencement** after permits and build-out (target 3–6 months free), not at signing.
4. **TI allowance** (or landlord work) for drains, plumbing and HVAC.
5. **Responsibility for HVAC replacement and maintenance** spelled out. AC runs constantly in Miami.
6. Permission for floor drains / trenching and for a generator pad.
7. **Outdoor area** rights (fenced yard, turf, shade).
8. Signage rights; parking and a drop-off zone.
9. Term 5–10 years with renewal options (the build-out needs a long payback). Assignment rights in case of sale.
10. Cap on personal guarantee (burn-off after X years); cap on NNN increases.
11. Restoration clause: limit the obligation to remove improvements at lease end.

## Red flags
No use contingency, residential walls adjoining, a flood-prone ground floor, landlord ban on outdoor areas, an HVAC system near end of life, a short term with no options.

## Output
A side-by-side comparison table, an effective-cost calculation, a list of negotiation asks, and red flags. Hand off to the attorney for final lease review.
