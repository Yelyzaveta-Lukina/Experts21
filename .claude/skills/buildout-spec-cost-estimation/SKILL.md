# Build-out specification & cost estimation

## Spec checklist
**Layout:** secure double-door entry (airlock) → lobby/retail → intake/eval room → 2–4 playrooms grouped by size and temperament (target ~50–75 sq ft of play area per dog) → rest/crate area → isolation room (separate air if possible) → grooming/bath → laundry → staff room → ADA restrooms → storage → optional outdoor yard.

**Plumbing and drainage:** trench or area drains in every playroom; floors sloped to the drains; connection to the **sanitary sewer** (not storm), with a solids/hair interceptor if required by WASD/DERM; hose bibs and hot water for wash-down.

**HVAC:** sized by a mechanical engineer for high fresh-air exchange, odour control and **dehumidification** (Miami humidity). Keep indoor temperature in the **50–85°F** range required by Miami-Dade Ch. 5; design for ~68–74°F. Use separate zones and a separate exhaust for isolation. Use MERV-rated filtration.

**Surfaces:** seamless epoxy or rubber flooring (non-slip, non-porous, ~$8–15/sq ft); FRP or epoxy-coated walls to ~4–5 ft; chew-proof corners; rounded edges.

**Sound:** acoustic panels in playrooms, insulated demising walls, and solid-core doors, especially near neighbours.

**Outdoor (optional):** K9 artificial turf with drainage and antimicrobial infill (~$15–25/sq ft), shade structures, misters, 6 ft+ fencing with a dig guard. Turf gets very hot in Miami, so shade is mandatory.

**Safety:** fire sprinklers and alarm as required for the change of occupancy, emergency lighting, a generator or transfer switch sized for HVAC and critical loads (hurricane outages), cameras, access control.

## Cost ranges (`docs/04-costs.md`, Estimates)
Build-out ~$35–90/sq ft for a flex/warehouse conversion (≈$210k–540k for 6,000 sq ft), plus flooring, yard, fixtures and equipment. A lean second-generation pet space costs much less. Add a **15–20% contingency** plus architect, engineering and permit fees (~$25–60k).

## Method
1. Confirm space parameters (sq ft, existing plumbing, HVAC tonnage and age, slab condition, ceiling height, electrical service).
2. Produce a room schedule and spec list as above.
3. Price each line (low/high) with its basis. Mark items the landlord's TI allowance could cover.
4. Flag permit triggers (change of use → fire, ADA, plumbing).
5. Recommend getting 2–3 GC bids with a standard scope.

## Output
A room schedule, spec table, cost table with contingency, permit triggers, and the list of questions for the GC and engineer.
