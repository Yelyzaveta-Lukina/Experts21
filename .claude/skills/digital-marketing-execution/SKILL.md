# Digital marketing execution

## Priorities (`docs/07-marketing.md`)
1. **Google Business Profile**: complete categories ("Dog day care center", "Pet boarding service", "Pet groomer"), hours, photos, EN/ES description, booking link, weekly posts, Q&A seeded.
2. **Local SEO**: a page per service and neighbourhood ("dog daycare Brickell", "doggy daycare Wynwood", "guardería canina Doral"); schema markup; NAP consistent on Yelp, BringFido, Apple Maps, Bing.
3. **Reviews**: ask at the moment of delight (first report card, a 10th visit) with a direct link. Target 4.8+ and 100 reviews in year 1. Respond to every review.
4. **Instagram/TikTok**: daily stories (playgroup clips, "pup of the day"), weekly reels (behind the scenes, staff, tips on heat and hurricanes), UGC reposts, local creators. Get photo consent through the client agreement.
5. **Paid**: Google Search ads (high intent) and Meta ads geo-fenced 3–5 miles around the site and target towers. Offer: free evaluation or a founding-member rate.
6. **Referral program** tracked in the booking software.
7. **Email/SMS**: onboarding sequence, package expiry nudges, win-back.

## Budget (Estimate)
Pre-launch $15–30k (brand, site, photography, launch event, ads). Ongoing **3–5% of revenue** (~$1.5–3k/month).

## Measurement
UTM links, call tracking, source captured at booking, and these KPIs: cost per lead, lead → evaluation → paying client conversion, **CAC**, payback, review count and rating, waitlist growth.

## Output
A channel plan with 90-day calendar, budget by channel, offers, KPIs and targets, and EN/ES keyword lists.
