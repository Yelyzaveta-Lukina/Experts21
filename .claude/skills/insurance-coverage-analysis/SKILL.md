# Insurance coverage analysis

> Research support. A licensed Florida agent or broker must quote and bind.

## Coverage map
| Coverage | What it covers | Notes for this business |
|---|---|---|
| General liability (GL) | Third-party bodily injury and property damage (e.g. a client is bitten in the lobby) | **Usually excludes injury to dogs in your care** |
| **Care, custody & control (animal bailee)** | Injury, illness or death of client dogs while in your care | Essential. Sample limits as low as $5k/year exist (Reported), so **buy meaningful per-animal and aggregate limits** |
| Professional liability | Errors in services (grooming, training) | Often bundled in pet-business policies |
| Property / BOP | Building improvements, equipment, inventory | Check **named-storm/hurricane % deductibles** (common in Florida) |
| Flood | Flood damage | Usually a separate policy (NFIP or private). Check the flood zone of the site |
| Business interruption / extra expense | Lost income during forced closures | Check whether outbreaks or civil-authority closures are covered (often excluded) |
| Workers' compensation | Staff injuries (bites, back strains) | **Required in Florida with 4+ employees (non-construction)**. Bites are a common claim |
| Commercial auto / hired & non-owned auto | Pet transport, errands | Needed if offering pickup and drop-off |
| Umbrella / excess | Extra limits over GL, auto, employer's liability | |
| EPLI | Employment claims | Useful with high turnover |
| Cyber | Client data, payments | Booking software holds personal data |

## Method
1. List the business facts that drive premiums: revenue, headcount, payroll, services (boarding increases risk), dogs per day, sq ft, location and flood zone, transport.
2. Recommend coverages and starting limits (Estimate, e.g. GL $1M/$2M; CCC per-animal limit high enough to cover serious vet care, ≥ $10–25k; umbrella $1–2M). Ask the broker to confirm what is customary.
3. Compare quotes in a table: carrier, AM Best rating, limits, deductibles (including the hurricane %), exclusions (communicable disease, intentional acts, breed exclusions), premium.
4. Check contract alignment: lease insurance requirements, client waiver, and vendor contracts.
5. Feed the premium into the financial model (`docs/04-costs.md` assumes $800–1,500/month).

## Output
A coverage table with recommended limits, a quote comparison, key exclusions to negotiate, and questions for the broker.
