# Tax setup & bookkeeping

> Research support. A Florida CPA must confirm.

## Registrations and taxes
| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Entity | An LLC is common. Choose default or S-corp tax treatment with the CPA. Florida corporate income tax (5.5%) applies to C-corps; **no Florida personal income tax** |
| Sunbiz annual report | Due 1 Jan–1 May each year |
| **Sales and use tax** (FDOR) | Retail product sales are taxable: **6% state + 1% Miami-Dade discretionary surtax = 7%**. **Confirm whether daycare, boarding and grooming services are taxable** (most Florida services are not) |
| Commercial rent | Florida's state sales tax on commercial rent was repealed effective 1 Oct 2025 (**verify, and check the lease for any pass-through**) |
| **Business tax receipts** | City **and** Miami-Dade County; renew by **30 Sep** each year |
| Tangible personal property tax | Annual DR-405 return to the Miami-Dade Property Appraiser for business equipment (due 1 April; small exemption available) |
| Payroll | Federal withholding, FICA, FUTA; Florida reemployment tax (FDOR) |
| 1099-NEC | For contractors (if any; see classification in `employment-wage-compliance`) |

## Chart of accounts (starter)
Revenue: daycare-memberships, daycare-packs/singles, boarding, grooming, training, retail, transport. COGS/direct: direct labour, supplies, retail COGS, card fees. Opex: rent, NNN/CAM, utilities, insurance, marketing, software, R&M, professional fees, licences and BTR. Below the line: interest, depreciation (build-out is usually depreciated as qualified improvement property; check bonus/§179 with the CPA).

## Month-end close checklist
Reconcile bank and card accounts → reconcile booking-software revenue against deposits (deferred revenue for unused packs and memberships) → payroll entries → accruals → sales tax return → KPI pack to the financial modeler.

## Compliance calendar
Monthly or quarterly sales tax · quarterly payroll (941, RT-6) · 1 Apr TPP return · 1 May Sunbiz report · 30 Sep BTR renewals · annual 940, W-2/1099 in January · income tax return.

## Output
A registration checklist, tax treatment notes with open questions for the CPA, chart of accounts, and the compliance calendar.
