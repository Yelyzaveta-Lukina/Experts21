## Profile template (one row per competitor)
`Name | Address / area | Drive time to our site | Services (daycare/boarding/grooming/training) | Hours | Single-day price | Pack and membership price | Assessment fee | Ratio claimed | Vaccine policy | Indoor/outdoor space | Webcams | Google rating (# reviews) | Strengths | Weaknesses | Source + date`

## Mystery-shop script (phone or web)
1. "I have a 2-year-old, 50 lb lab mix and need care about 3 days a week near ___. What are your options and prices?"
2. "What do you need before the first day? Is there an evaluation, and what does it cost?"
3. "How many dogs per staff member in a playgroup? Are small dogs separated?"
4. "What are your hours? Are there fees for late pickup?"
5. "Which vaccines do you require (Bordetella, flu, lepto)?"
6. "Do you have webcams or send updates?"
7. "Is there a waitlist?" (A waitlist is a strong demand signal.)

## Analysis
- Plot each competitor on **price vs. perceived quality/safety** and **location vs. our candidate sites**.
- Use review count as a proxy for volume. Look for recurring complaints (fights, smell, staff turnover, sick dogs) as openings.
- Conclude with: white space, threats, and the price corridor available to us.
