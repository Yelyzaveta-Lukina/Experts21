# Florida & Miami-Dade regulatory lookup

## Step 1: pin down the jurisdiction
Identify the parcel's municipality (City of Miami, Doral, Coral Gables, Miami Beach, North Miami, Aventura, etc.) or whether it is **unincorporated Miami-Dade**. Zoning, the Certificate of Use and the city business tax receipt come from the municipality. Animal standards (Ch. 5), DERM and the county business tax receipt are countywide.

## Step 3: what we already know (see `docs/05-permits-licenses.md`)
- Miami-Dade Animal Services says that, after recent state legislation, it **no longer licenses pet care centers or kennel operators**, but it still applies Chapter 5 standards. The specific state bill has not been identified. **Verify.**
- Ch. 5 pet care center: daytime-only, air-conditioned indoor facility. Indoor temperature **50–85°F**. **No dogs with known or suspected communicable disease**. Clean each enclosure daily. **Emergency medical release form** from every owner.
- Overnight boarding is likely a "kennel", not a "pet care center". The classification matters for zoning.
- Rabies vaccination is required for dogs aged 4 months and older (F.S. 828.30). Miami-Dade requires a dog licence tag at 4+ months.
- Dog owners are strictly liable for bites (F.S. 767.04).
- A business inside a municipality needs both a city **and** a county business tax receipt. The tax year runs 1 Oct–30 Sep.
