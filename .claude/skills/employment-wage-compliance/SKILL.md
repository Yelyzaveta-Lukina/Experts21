# Employment & wage compliance

> Research support. Confirm with an employment attorney or payroll provider.

## Key rules (verify current values)
| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Florida minimum wage | **$14.00 from 30 Sep 2025; $15.00 from 30 Sep 2026** (Amendment 2). From 2027 it is adjusted annually for inflation (Verified schedule) |
| Tipped employees | Florida allows a tip credit (cash wage = minimum − $3.02). Only relevant if staff receive tips |
| Overtime (FLSA) | 1.5× regular rate above 40 hours per workweek for non-exempt staff |
| Exempt status | Managers must meet the duties test **and** the salary threshold (the federal level has been in flux after litigation. **Verify the current figure**) |
| Workers' comp | Required for non-construction employers with **4+ employees** (F.S. ch. 440) |
| Reemployment (unemployment) tax | Register with Florida DOR when payroll begins |
| I-9 | All hires |
| E-Verify | Florida requires it for private employers with **25+ employees** (2023 law) |
| Minors | Florida child labour rules on hours and hazardous work. Check before hiring under-18s |
| Posters | Federal and Florida required workplace posters |
| Payroll tax | FICA, FUTA, federal withholding. **Florida has no personal state income tax** |

## Worker classification
Groomers, trainers and bathers are often paid on commission or work as "booth renters". Misclassifying them as contractors is a common risk. Apply the IRS and DOL tests (control over how, when and where the work is done; tools; integration into the business; opportunity for profit or loss). Default to employee unless the arrangement truly is independent.

## Method
1. List the roles, pay types and expected hours.
2. Check each against the table, flagging exempt/non-exempt and employee/contractor status.
3. Compute the effect of the wage floor on the labour budget (hand off to the staffing model and the financial modeler).
4. List the registrations and documents needed before the first payroll.

A compliance checklist with status, risks, and the questions for an attorney or payroll provider.
