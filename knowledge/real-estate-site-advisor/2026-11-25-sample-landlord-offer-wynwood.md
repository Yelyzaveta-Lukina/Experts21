# SAMPLE landlord offer (training example): 2150 NW 3rd Ave, Wynwood, Unit B (fictional address)

Space: 6,200 sq ft single-storey warehouse/flex space (former furniture showroom), plus an 1,800 sq ft fenced rear lot
Proposed use (as written by landlord): "pet grooming and pet supply retail"
Base rent: $26 per sq ft per year, NNN
NNN charges (taxes, insurance, common area): $7.50 per sq ft per year (estimated, not capped)
Term: 7 years, no renewal option offered
Annual increases: 4% per year
Free rent: 2 months, starting at lease signing
Tenant improvement allowance: $15 per sq ft
HVAC: tenant responsible for all repairs and replacement (existing rooftop units installed 2009)
Outdoor lot: landlord approval required for any animal use of the outdoor area
Security deposit: 3 months of rent
Personal guarantee: required for the full term
Exclusive use: none offered (another pet business could open in the building)
Restoration: tenant must remove all improvements (including floor drains) at lease end
Zoning / permit contingency: none offered
Offer expires: December 15, 2026
