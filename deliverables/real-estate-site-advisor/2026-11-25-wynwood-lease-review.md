# Rosa (real-estate-site-advisor): Wynwood Unit B lease review

Date: 2026-11-25. Question: "Should we sign this Wynwood lease as written? What are the top three things to negotiate?"
Input: the SAMPLE (training) offer and listing in `knowledge/real-estate-site-advisor/`.

**Bottom line:** No, don't sign as written. The use clause doesn't allow dog daycare, there is no zoning or permit contingency, and the all-in cost of $33.50/sq ft is well above the ≤ $25 trigger. Counter before the 15 Dec 2026 expiry.

## Top 3 to negotiate
1. **Use clause and zoning/permit contingency**
   - Use clause: "dog daycare, boarding, grooming, training and related retail, including inherent noise and odour".
   - Contingency: the tenant may terminate and recover the deposit if a written Miami 21 use determination, building and fire permits, DERM/WASD approval and the Certificate of Use are not obtained within 120 days.
   - The apartment building ~30 ft from the rear lot raises the risk of a hearing and of complaints.
2. **Rent start and economics**
   - Rent starts at the Certificate of Use, or 6 months after permits, whichever is first.
   - Base rent $21–22/sq ft.
   - NNN capped at $6.50, with a 3% cap on controllable increases.
   - 3% annual increases.
   - TI of $40+/sq ft (no floor drains; build-out ~$35–90/sq ft).
3. **HVAC, term and guarantee**
   - The landlord replaces or funds the 2009 HVAC units, or at least bears capital replacement.
   - Two 5-year renewal options.
   - Personal guarantee burns off after 24 months and is capped at 12 months' rent.
   - No obligation to remove improvements (such as floor drains) at lease end.
