# 2. Target Customers

*Most of this file is **Estimate**: working personas built from the market data in [01-market.md](01-market.md) and from industry practice. Test them with surveys and pre-launch sign-ups.*

## Primary segments

### A. Urban young professionals ("Brickell Professional")
- **Who:** aged 25–40, single or a couple, no children. Works in finance, tech, law or healthcare. Lives in a high-rise rental or condo in Brickell, Downtown, Edgewater, Wynwood or Midtown.
- **Dog:** often a young, high-energy dog (doodle, retriever, frenchie, shepherd mix), frequently 1–3 years old.
- **Need:** somewhere for the dog to burn off energy while they work 9–10 hour days, and relief from guilt about leaving it alone.
- **Buys:** 2–5 days a week on a monthly membership. Adds baths and grooming, and boarding when travelling.
- **Values:** convenience (early drop-off, late pickup, on the commute, valet or easy parking), webcams, photo updates on Instagram, a premium feel.
- **Price tolerance:** high. $40–$50/day is acceptable if the service is good.

### B. Suburban families ("Gables / Doral Family")
- **Who:** aged 35–55 with children and a house. Lives in Coral Gables, South Miami, Doral, Kendall, Pinecrest or Miami Lakes.
- **Dog:** medium or large dogs, sometimes two.
- **Need:** occasional daycare, care during holidays and travel, grooming, and socialisation for a new puppy.
- **Buys:** packs of 10 or 20 days, boarding, grooming, training classes.
- **Values:** trust, cleanliness, a vet-like level of safety, easy parking, Spanish-speaking staff (Doral, Hialeah, Kendall).
- **Price tolerance:** medium. Uses multi-dog discounts.

### C. Puppy owners
- **Who:** anyone with a dog aged 4–12 months.
- **Need:** socialisation, help with house-training and basic manners, an outlet for energy.
- **Buys:** a puppy program (half-days plus training). This becomes a gateway to a long-term membership.
- **Note:** dogs must have the full vaccination series before joining group play. A strong acquisition channel when partnered with vets and breeders.

### D. Travellers and seasonal residents
- **Who:** snowbirds, frequent business travellers, tourists who travel with their dogs.
- **Buys:** boarding plus daytime play. Higher revenue per stay, but seasonal (peaks in winter and holidays).
- **Only relevant if the business offers overnight boarding** (see the licensing note in [05-permits-licenses.md](05-permits-licenses.md): overnight care changes how the business is classified).

### E. Senior, special-needs and small-dog owners
- **Who:** owners of small breeds, senior dogs or anxious dogs that do not fit large group play.
- **Buys:** a separate "small and calm" playroom, half-days, or enrichment sessions.
- **Opportunity:** many low-cost competitors do not separate small dogs well, so this can set the business apart.

## Who is *not* the target
- Owners whose dogs are aggressive toward other dogs or are not neutered or spayed (industry standard: intact dogs older than about 6–7 months are excluded from group play).
- Highly price-sensitive owners who are well served by Rover sitters at about $30/day.

## Geographic priorities (Estimate)

| Rank | Area | Why | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brickell / Downtown / Edgewater** | Dense, high income, many dogs, long work hours | Very expensive space, little industrial zoning, parking and drop-off logistics |
| 2 | **Wynwood / Midtown / Little River / Allapattah** | Industrial and warehouse stock near dense residential areas; on the commute | Existing competitors in Wynwood (D.O.G., freeDOGm/Cadence K9) |
| 3 | **Doral** | Fast-growing families and young professionals, plenty of flex and warehouse space | Established local players (Bark Square, Doggies Gone Wild) |
| 4 | **Coral Gables / South Miami** | Affluent families | Strict zoning and aesthetic rules; D.O.G. Coral Gables |
| 5 | **Aventura / North Miami Beach** | Affluent condo dwellers | D.O.G. Aventura |

## What owners say they care about (industry pattern, Reported)
1. **Safety**: supervision, staff-to-dog ratios, temperament screening, how fights are handled.
2. **Cleanliness and smell**, and visible health protocols (vaccine checks).
3. **Staff who visibly love dogs** and send updates (photos, report cards, webcams).
4. **Convenience**: hours (7am–7pm or later), location, parking, online booking.
5. **Price and flexibility**: packages, no long lock-in.

## Open questions
- Run a survey of 200+ local owners (Instagram, dog parks, condo buildings) to test willingness to pay at $35, $42 and $50 per day, and which days they need care.
- Find out the share of hybrid workers in the target ZIP codes, and which days of the week they go to the office.
- Decide whether to offer overnight boarding (affects segment D, licensing, staffing and insurance).
