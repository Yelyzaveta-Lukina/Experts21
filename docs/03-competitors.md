# 3. Competitors

The Miami market is **fragmented**. It has many independents, a few local multi-site brands, national franchises, and app-based individual sitters. The list below comes from directories, review sites and press (**Reported**). Hours, prices and whether each business is still open must be checked before relying on them.

Dogtopia operates elsewhere in Florida; its Fort Myers location charges about $39/day on a wellness plan and $45–47/day on prepaid passes. **Action:** search each brand's location finder for Miami-Dade ZIP codes.

## Substitutes
- **Rover / Wag sitters (in-home daycare):** average about **$38/day** in Miami (Rover, Sept 2025; range ~$30–45). The national average is $40.48 and puppies cost ~$42.81. Some North Miami sitters start at $29/day. These are low-cost and flexible, but have no supervised group play and safety is uneven.
- **Dog walkers** (midday walks at ~$20–30 per walk): cheaper for owners whose dog only needs a break.
- **Public dog parks** (free) and **dog-friendly offices**.
- **Friends and family** (APPA: about 4 in 10 owners use them when travelling).

Industry-wide revenue per dog-day: **$25–$46** (vendor blogs, Reported).

## Typical pricing structures in the market
- Single day, half-day, packs of 5/10/20 days (5–20% discount), monthly unlimited or fixed days-per-week memberships.
- One-off temperament assessment or "trial day" (often free, or $0–$40).
- Add-ons: bath on pickup, nail trim, enrichment or training sessions, webcam access, transport (pickup and drop-off).
- Multi-dog discount (10–15%).

## Where a new entrant can stand out (Estimate)
1. **Location and convenience**: a site with a drive-through or valet drop-off on a main commuter route, open 6:30/7am–7:30pm.
2. **Safety transparency**: published staff-to-dog ratio, certified staff (e.g. Dog Daycare Academy / PACCC), daily report cards, live cams.
3. **Indoor climate-controlled play** suited to Miami's heat and rain, with outdoor sessions only at cooler times of day.
4. **Size and temperament groups**, including a small and senior dog room.
5. **Bilingual (English/Spanish) service** and a strong social media presence.
6. **Memberships** that lock in recurring revenue.

## Open questions
- Mystery-shop or call 8–10 competitors to record current prices, hours, ratios, vaccine policy, capacity and waitlists.
- Map all competitors against shortlisted sites, with drive times.
- Check Google review counts and ratings as a proxy for each competitor's volume and reputation.
- Confirm which national franchises are present or planned in Miami-Dade.
