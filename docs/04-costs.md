| **Total** | **≈ $165k–$370k** | **≈ $525k–$1.2M** | A franchise is comparable or higher, plus the fees |

**Takeaways**
- Finding **second-generation space** (a former vet clinic, kennel or daycare) or getting a large landlord TI allowance cuts the build-out cost substantially.
- The building conditions that drive cost are: **floor drains connected to the sanitary sewer, high-capacity HVAC (air changes and dehumidification), noise insulation, and the change-of-use fire and life-safety upgrades.**
- Financing: SBA 7(a) loans are common. A franchise brand makes lenders more comfortable. Lenders typically want 10–30% equity injection.

## Unit economics and break-even (Estimate)

| Scenario | Avg dogs/day | Realised price/day (after discounts) | Daycare revenue | Add-ons (+15%: grooming, baths, retail, training) | Total revenue | Approx. operating profit (before debt) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ramp-up (months 1–6) | 25 | $38 | $20.6k | $3.1k | **$23.7k** | **−$22k to −$25k** |
| Break-even | ~55 | $38 | $45.4k | $6.8k | **$52.2k** | ≈ $0 |
| Target | 65 | $42 | $59.2k | $8.9k | **$68.1k** | **+$13k to +$17k** (≈20–25%) |
| Target + boarding (15 nights/day at $65) | 65 | $42 | $59.2k + $29.6k boarding | $8.9k | **$97.7k** | +$30k or more (with more labour and overnight staff) |

**What the model says**
1. Daycare alone at Miami prices is **a high-volume, thin-margin business**. You need about **55+ dogs a day** to break even at a 6,000 sq ft site, and 65+ to reach the 20% margins reported for good operators.
2. **Boarding, grooming and training are where the margin comes from.** Most successful facilities combine all four services.
3. **Labour is about 50% of revenue** and is the main lever. Ratio policy, scheduling to match drop-off and pickup peaks, and an owner who works on site make a large difference.
4. **The ramp-up burns cash.** Assume 9–18 months to reach steady utilisation, so the working capital reserve is essential.
5. A franchise adds 9% of revenue in fees (7% royalty + 2% brand fund), about $6k/month at target volume. This must be paid for by faster ramp-up and stronger brand pricing.

## KPIs for the finance agent to track
- Average dogs per day and utilisation (target 75–80%+ of capacity)
- Revenue per dog-day, including add-ons
- Labour % of revenue (target ≤ 45%)
- Membership share of revenue (recurring) and monthly churn
- Customer acquisition cost and payback
- Boarding occupancy and average daily rate (if offered)
