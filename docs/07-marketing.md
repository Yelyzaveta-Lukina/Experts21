# 7. Marketing

*This file is mostly an **Estimate**: a working go-to-market plan built from the customer and competitor research in [02-target-customers.md](02-target-customers.md) and [03-competitors.md](03-competitors.md).*

## Positioning options

| Option | Promise | Price point | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. "Safe, transparent, climate-smart" premium daycare** *(recommended starting point)* | Small supervised groups, published ratios, certified staff, live cams, air-conditioned indoor play built for Miami's heat | $42–$50/day; memberships ~$36–$42/day | Brickell / Wynwood / Edgewater professionals |
| B. Neighbourhood value daycare | Clean, friendly, affordable | $30–$38/day | Doral / Kendall families; competes with Rover |
| C. Resort / lifestyle | Pool, spa, grooming, boarding, social-media-friendly | $50+/day | Aventura / Coral Gables; competes with D.O.G. |

## Sample price sheet (Estimate, for testing)

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Temperament evaluation / first half-day | Free or $25 (credited against a package) |
| Full day (single) | $45 |
| Half day (≤5h) | $32 |
| 10-day pack (valid 90 days) | $400 ($40/day) |
| 20-day pack | $760 ($38/day) |
| Membership 2 days/week | ~$330/month |
| Membership 3 days/week | ~$470/month |
| Membership 5 days/week (unlimited) | ~$720/month |
| Additional dog in the same household | −15% |
| Add-ons | Bath on pickup $25–$45, nail trim $15, 15-min enrichment/training $15–$20, pickup and drop-off $15–$25 each way |
| Boarding (if offered) | $60–$75/night incl. daycare |

Make memberships the default offer: they smooth demand, improve retention and turn revenue into recurring revenue.

## Pre-launch (3–4 months before opening)
1. **Brand and website** in English and Spanish with online booking (through the booking software), vaccine upload, and a clear safety page.
2. **Google Business Profile** set up early, with photos of the construction. Do local SEO for "dog daycare Brickell", "doggy daycare Wynwood" and similar searches.
3. **Founding member waitlist:** discounted first month or locked-in rates for the first 100 sign-ups. Use the waitlist to test demand before committing to staff levels.
4. **Instagram and TikTok**: behind-the-scenes posts of the build-out, staff introductions, dog "report cards". Miami is a very Instagram-driven market.
5. **Partnerships:**
   - **Luxury residential buildings:** resident perks, lobby events, building-wide discounts, pickup shuttles from towers.
   - **Veterinary clinics, groomers, trainers, pet stores** (referral cards, cross-promotions).
   - **Employers** with dog-owning staff (perk programs), coworking spaces.
   - Breeders and rescues: puppy and adoption welcome packs.
6. **Open house / "yappy hour"** launch event with local influencers and pet-friendly restaurants.

## Ongoing acquisition channels (in rough priority order)
1. Google search and Maps (reviews are critical: ask every happy customer)
2. Referral program (e.g. a free day for both the referrer and the new client)
3. Instagram / TikTok organic posts plus geo-targeted paid ads (radius of 3–5 miles)
4. Partnerships with buildings and vets
5. Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, neighbourhood events and dog parks
6. Listings on Yelp, BringFido and Care.com business directories
7. Rover/Wag: *not* a primary channel. They compete on price.

## Retention
- Daily **report cards and photos**, live webcams, a birthday treat, and personal updates for the dog's "best friends".
- Pausable memberships (for travel), loyalty rewards, quick and transparent incident communication.
- Track **monthly churn**, NPS, and review rating (target 4.8+ on Google).

## Marketing budget (Estimate)
- Pre-launch: $15k–$30k (brand, website, photography, launch event, ads).
- Ongoing: **3–5% of revenue** (~$1.5k–$3k/month), weighted toward Google, social media and referral credits.

## Open questions
- Test positioning A vs B with the waitlist and a survey (price sensitivity at $35/$42/$50).
- Choose a brand name and check trademark availability (USPTO, Sunbiz) and the domain and social handles.
- Shortlist 10 luxury buildings near the chosen site for partnership outreach.
